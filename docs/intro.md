# Candi TMS User Guide

# Welcome to Candi TMS

**Candi TMS** is the **Tracking & Monitoring System** operated by **PT. Candi Solusi Angkasa**. It provides a centralized platform for managing delivery orders, driver dispatch, and last-mile logistics operations.

---

## 🔐 System Login

To access Candi TMS, open your web browser and navigate to:
`https://tmsmsa.com/candi/tms_login.php`

![TMS Login Screen](/img/tms_login_screen.png)

### Login Steps

1. **Email Address:** Enter your registered Candi account email address in the email input field.
2. **Password:** Type your account password in the password field.
3. **Sign In:** Click the **Login** button to authenticate and enter your dashboard.

:::tip Access Issues
If you are unable to log in, contact your system administrator to verify your account credentials or reset your password.
:::
