# Price

The **Price** module defines the rates for each contract by route (origin → destination), product, and contract type. These rates are used automatically when calculating shipment costs and selling prices during booking.

Open it from the sidebar: **Master Data ➔ Contract Data ➔ Price**.

![Price List](/img/tms_price_main.png)

---

## 📋 The Price List

The table shows every price entry with its contract, type, product, route, cost, and selling price. Use **Search** to filter and the **Action** buttons to **Edit** or **Delete** an entry.

---

## ➕ Adding a New Price

1. Click the **Add Price** button.
2. Fill in the **Add Price** form:

   | Field | Description |
   |---|---|
   | **Contract** | Select the parent contract this rate belongs to. |
   | **Contract Type** | The category/type of the contract. |
   | **Contract Product** | The specific product or service covered by the rate. |
   | **Origin** | Origin location of the route. |
   | **Destination** | Destination location of the route. |
   | **Cost** | Your internal/base cost for the route. |
   | **Price** | The selling price charged to the customer. |

3. Click **Save changes**.

---

## ✏️ Editing & 🗑️ Deleting

- Click **Edit** on a row to adjust an existing rate, then **Save changes**.
- Click **Delete** to remove a rate; confirm when prompted.

:::tip
To quickly look up an applicable rate without browsing the whole list, use the [Price Check](./price-check.md) tool.
:::
